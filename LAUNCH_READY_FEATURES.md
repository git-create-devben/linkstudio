- ❌ Custom Domain Support (DNS management complexity)
- ❌ Email Capture (GDPR compliance, email service integration)
- ❌ WhatsApp Integration (API complexity)
- ❌ Mini-Shop (E-commerce complexity)
- ❌ Custom CSS (Security concerns)
- ❌ SLA Guarantees (Can't provide as solo dev)

### **Why These Were Removed**
1. **Time to Market** - Focus on core value proposition
2. **Maintenance Burden** - Reduce ongoing support complexity
3. **Security Concerns** - Avoid potential vulnerabilities
4. **Resource Constraints** - Solo developer limitations
